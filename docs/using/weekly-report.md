# The weekly report

Every Sunday between 6 and 7 PM, Convex writes a short report on each laptop's week and sends it to everyone in `TELEGRAM_ADMIN_IDS`. The time zone is `REPORT_TIMEZONE` on the Convex deployment, `America/New_York` by default. Without Telegram, reports are still saved and shown on the admin app's Reports page.

It's built from the week's events, so it covers time the laptop was offline once the queued events arrive.

## What it says

In this order, leaving out lines with nothing to say:

- which days she used it, or that she didn't use it at all;
- the tiles she opened most;
- how often she asked for help;
- how many reminders came up and how many she answered with OK;
- how often the internet went down, and for how long in all;
- how often the Wi-Fi signal was weak;
- how long the laptop wasn't heard from, whether asleep, off or offline;
- how often the battery got down to 10% or less;
- how often the screen was locked.

Then either "Nothing unusual." or a **Worth a look** list:

- any stretch of more than 24 hours without hearing from the laptop;
- times Get help didn't reach you, so she saw your phone number instead;
- more than seven startups in the week.

## Reading it

The report tells you how the setup is going, not how she is. A week with no help presses and steady use is good news. A week of silence might mean the laptop is off in a drawer, that she's away, or that the Wi-Fi changed. Call her before you debug anything.

## This week so far

The Reports page has **This week so far**, which shows the last 7 days as the report would read right now. Use it after a change to see whether things improved without waiting for Sunday.
